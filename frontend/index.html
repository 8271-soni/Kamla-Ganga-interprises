About the Company

Engineering discipline, dressed in good design.

Kamla Ganga Interprises Pvt. Ltd. is an architecture and construction consultancy built around one idea: a building should be as sound in its structural calculations as it is beautiful on the elevation sheet. We plan, design, engineer and estimate — for private homes, commercial developments and government works alike.

  • ✓Vision. To be the most trusted planning-to-execution partner for Indian homeowners, developers and government bodies.
  • ✓Mission. Deliver every drawing, estimate and structure with technical accuracy, transparent costing and on-time execution.
